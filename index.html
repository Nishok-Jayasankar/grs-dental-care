Three things we do differently

Not promises. Just how the clinic runs.

The same dentist, every visit

You are not passed between whoever is free. The person who examines you is the person who treats you and remembers your history.

The price before the procedure

You get a written estimate before anything starts, including what a treatment plan costs if it runs across several visits.

Instruments sealed until you sit down

Everything is autoclaved and stays in its pouch until you are in the chair. Ask to see it opened — people do, and that is fine.

What we treat

Routine care and most common procedures, all under one roof.

  • Check-up and X-ray

    A full look at teeth, gums and bite, with a digital X-ray if something needs confirming. Where every new patient starts.

  • Cleaning and polishing

    Scaling to remove tartar and stains, usually in a single half-hour sitting. Recommended once or twice a year.

  • Fillings

    Tooth-coloured composite fillings for decay and chips, matched to the shade of the tooth beside it.

  • Root canal treatment

    For a tooth that has become infected or painfully sensitive. Usually one or two sittings, with a crown afterwards.

  • Crowns and bridges

    Caps for weakened or root-treated teeth, and bridges to close a gap. Ceramic and metal-ceramic options.

  • Braces and clear aligners

    Straightening for crowded or gapped teeth, in metal, ceramic or removable clear aligners.
