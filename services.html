What we treat

Everyday dental care and most common procedures, done in the clinic rather than referred out. If you are unsure which of these you need, book a check-up and we will tell you.

Routine care

The visits that keep everything else off this page.

  • Check-up and X-ray

    A full look at teeth, gums and bite, with a digital X-ray where something needs confirming. Takes about twenty minutes.

  • Cleaning and polishing (scaling)

    Removes the hardened tartar a toothbrush cannot reach, which is what causes bleeding gums and bad breath. Once or twice a year for most people.

  • Fillings

    Tooth-coloured composite fillings for decay, chips and worn edges, shade-matched to the tooth beside it.

  • Teeth whitening

    In-clinic whitening for staining from coffee, tea, tobacco or age. We will tell you honestly whether it will work on your teeth before you pay for it.

When a tooth is damaged

Treatment for pain, infection and teeth that are past saving with a filling.

  • Root canal treatment

    For a tooth that has become infected or severely sensitive. Usually one or two sittings, followed by a crown so the tooth does not crack later.

  • Crowns and bridges

    A crown caps a weakened or root-treated tooth. A bridge closes a gap using the teeth on either side. Ceramic and metal-ceramic options at different price points.

  • Extractions and wisdom teeth

    Removal where a tooth cannot be saved, including impacted wisdom teeth. Done under local anaesthetic, with aftercare instructions you can actually follow.

  • Dental implants

    A permanent replacement for a missing tooth, fixed into the jaw rather than resting on the gum. Treatment runs across several months.

Straightening and children

Longer courses of treatment, planned around school and work.

  • Braces and clear aligners

    For crowded, gapped or protruding teeth. Metal braces, ceramic braces, or removable clear aligners if you would rather they were not visible.

  • Children's dentistry

    Check-ups, cleaning, fluoride, and fillings for milk teeth. First visit is usually just a look and a chat, so the chair stops being frightening.

Not sure what you need?

Most people booking their first visit here are not sure either. Start with a check-up — you will leave knowing what is wrong, what it costs and how urgent it is.
