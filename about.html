A small clinic, run deliberately that way.

GRS Dental Care is a single-chair practice on Ponniamman Kovil 1st Street in Sholinganallur. One dentist, one room, and enough time in each appointment to explain what is happening.

Why it is set up like this

Large dental chains run on volume. You see whoever is on shift, the consultation is short, and the treatment plan arrives as a number at the end. It works, but a lot of people leave unsure what was actually done to them.

We went the other way. Fewer patients in a day, the same dentist each time, and a written estimate before anything begins. If a tooth can wait six months, we will say so rather than book you in next week.

The room you see here is the room you will be treated in. Everything that goes into your mouth comes out of an autoclave pouch opened in front of you.

What a first visit looks like

So there are no surprises.

We look and we ask

A full examination of teeth, gums and bite, plus questions about pain, habits and anything you have had done before. X-ray only if it changes the answer.

We explain what we found

In plain language, with the X-ray on screen if there is one. Including which problems are urgent and which can safely wait.

You get the costs in writing

A written plan with the price of each step before anything starts. You are free to take it away and think about it.
